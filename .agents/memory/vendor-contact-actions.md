---
name: Vendor contact actions
description: Contact directory vendors without assuming they are workspace chat members.
---

Vendor messaging uses the recorded contact email. Do not infer a workspace chat recipient from a vendor ID, company name, or contact name.

**Why:** Directory vendors are not guaranteed to have workspace user accounts. Guessing an identity could send a private message to the wrong person. A vendor without a contact email has no actionable messaging destination.

**How to apply:** Keep contact actions explicit about their destination. Any future in-app vendor messaging must introduce an explicit, authorized vendor-to-user association first.
