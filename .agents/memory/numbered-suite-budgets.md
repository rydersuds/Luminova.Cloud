description: Why requests and trade limits follow individual numbered units rather than dwelling-type groups.
---

The user wants trade budgets to match the per-unit budget breakdown and requests broken down by individual numbered suites/units, not dwelling-type groups. Register real suite labels and map them to their source dwelling types; keep shared-area costs separate.

**Why:** The user explicitly selected “By individual numbered suite/unit” when asked how requests should be allocated.

**How to apply:** Use the dwelling type's approved internal trade subtotals for each mapped suite. Do not invent suite numbers from quantities, divide project-wide trade totals equally, or guess historical suite assignments. Historical unallocated costs remain explicit and must not create reliable-looking suite allowances. Aggregate new suite expenses upward for project reporting without distributing old costs downward.
